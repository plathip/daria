On the drone: essential

Frame + 4 motors + ESC
5" quad, 6S; must lift itself + 0.5 kg ball (≈1.3 kg all-up).
Flight controller supported by ArduPilot
e.g. SpeedyBee F405 V5. Betaflight-only boards can't be converted.
GPS with compass
Position and heading. Without both there is no autonomy.
RC transmitter + receiver
Your override at all times; the mode switch beats the computer.
Bluetooth (built into the FC) or a telemetry radio
How this laptop talks to the drone during operations. Only needed to start a mission; the flight itself runs inside the aircraft.
Dropper + positional servo
3D-printed pin-pull with a standard (not 360°) MG90S on FC output 6.
Fire-extinguisher ball ≤ 0.5 kg, 6S battery + charger

On the drone: optional

FPV camera + video transmitter + goggles
Not needed to fly autonomously. Only for flying manually beyond what your eyes can see.
Buzzer / LEDs
Find a landed drone, hear arming tones.

USB vs Bluetooth

The USB cable is for setup only (flashing, parameters, calibration). Operations use Bluetooth, and nothing is plugged into the drone when it flies.
